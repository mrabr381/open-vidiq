    How the maths works

    • Median: the middle view count of the 20 videos (average of the 10th and 11th when sorted). Exact median is 2,333,635.5 views; the headline shows it truncated to 2,333,635, as in the README.
    • Outlier score: a video's views ÷ the median. 1.00× is exactly average; 2.00× or more is an outlier.
    • Velocity: views ÷ whole days since publish, counted to the snapshot date (19 Nov 2026) — so a 9-day-old hit can be fairly compared with a 2-month-old one. Days are counted the same way as openvidiq/analysis.py (days_since, minimum 1).
    • Short vs long-form: in this sample the 13 Shorts are all under 90 seconds and the 7 long-form videos are 3:34 or longer.
