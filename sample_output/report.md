## Method & limitations

- Outlier score, velocity, keywords and patterns are computed from the 24 videos in this report — nothing is estimated.
- Views are a snapshot at generation time; a video published yesterday has had less time to accumulate views than a year-old one — velocity (views/day) corrects for that, outlier score does not.
- The free YouTube Data API tier allows 10,000 quota units/day; this tool uses ~1 unit per video fetched (search, when needed, costs 100).
- Subscriber counts are hidden on some channels — views-per-subscriber is then unavailable, and outlier score (median-based) is used instead.
- Generated titles are suggestions built from your real keywords — they are not predictions and carry no view guarantee.
